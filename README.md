<p align="justify">
In a land where twilight reigns and the sun dares not rise, a knight named Zulkifli roams the ancient paths. Clad in obsidian armor and bound by oaths lost to time, he seeks no crown, no glory — only the truth buried beneath shadow and ruin. His blade whispers forgotten prayers, and his steps echo like the toll of bells in a long-dead cathedral.
</p>

<p align="justify">
He rides through ravaged forests where trees weep black sap, and ruins where spirits cling to stone. The wind speaks his name not in fear, but in reverence. For he is not merely a wanderer — he is a memory made flesh, a question the world dares not answer. Wherever he goes, darkness lingers, not as an enemy, but as a companion.
</p>

<p align="justify">
There are no songs sung of Zulkifli. Only scars in the stone, runes carved in bone, and eyes that remember when the stars bled. Every town he leaves behind speaks of a shadowed figure who asked nothing, helped many, and vanished with the dawn. To him, the journey is sacred — a pilgrimage into the unknown, guided by silence and steel.
</p>

<p align="justify">
To be a knight is not to conquer, but to endure. Zulkifli does not flee from death — he rides beside it. And though his path is cursed with solitude, the fire in his heart burns against the cold of fate. He is a knight of the dusk, a blade in the dark, a soul that walks even when all light is lost.
</p>
